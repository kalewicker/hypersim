# Selfmod v19b — a further edit survives funded reproduction

The second-child families meet the unchanged durability criterion in **8/8
fresh worlds after the further edit**, exceeding the fixed 6/8 threshold.
Faithful second-child inheritance also passes **8/8**. The original prune
family and all three culture members survive in every branch, and the original
families also pass durability 8/8. All 16 full 10,000-step treatment trajectories
complete without enumeration censoring or missing worlds.

This supports one further externally selected edit at a naturally funded birth
within an established prune family, followed by durable faithful inheritance
under the stated native financing and population bound. It does not demonstrate
superiority over faithful inheritance, autonomous choice of edits, indefinite
viability, or an open-ended sequence of beneficial modifications.

The control establishes the founder-family benchmark in this panel. There are
no paired durability gains or losses. Smaller birth, productivity and capital
differences are descriptive; eight paired worlds do not establish equivalence,
significance, or a universal ordering of the genotypes.

V19b prospectively changes the intervention definition. After the native paid
phase creates the selected child, a deterministic adapter finds the unique
`HAS_PNODE` constraint with `K_VAR_ID=0`, derives its index in unchanged native
order, and substitutes only parameter 0 of authenticated `op03_member0_draw1`.
The other four parameters remain unchanged. It applies unchanged native
operator 3 once, consumes no scheduler draw, and archives both parameter vectors
and the complete material order. The result must be the exact executable
composite with labels [null,1], required LHS edge [0,1], creation of label2,
and pruned RHS edge [2,1], class hash **4713340462273116244**. No repair or
alternate edit is attempted on a wrong genotype.

The correction mattered in this new panel: native child order was [var1,var0]
at **359, 373, 379, 397 and 401**, and [var0,var1] at the other three seeds.
Every edited child nevertheless realizes the same fixed semantic composite.
This is a revised semantic intervention, not the unchanged original recorded
five-parameter draw.

## What this warrants next

Continue the established prune platform and this precise further-edit variant
as candidates for a prospectively fixed serial-edit experiment. This panel
demonstrates two introduced edits separated by native faithful reproduction,
with the second founder's own descendants passing the fixed finite horizon.
It supplies a valid paired faithful control and a durable twice-edited genotype.
Any third edit should be named and semantically targeted before new output,
introduced at another naturally funded birth, and judged on its own descendant
family with unchanged accounting and an explicit control. Current results do
not justify broad mutation search, autonomous optimization or training admission.
No third-edit experiment or training run was launched, and root ERE work was
not modified by this isolated LK assay.
