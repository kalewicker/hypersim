# Selfmod v20 — the remaining label constraint marks a durability boundary

Removing the remaining var1/label1 constraint at a third funded birth produces
**0/8 durable third-child families**, against **6/8 under faithful inheritance**.
The faithful control meets the prospectively fixed 6/8 benchmark. Seven worlds
reach the paired third-birth intervention; all seven edited third families become
extinct before the 2,000-step warmup ends. Five edited cultures subsequently lose
every rule root. The eighth world, seed 419, never triggers the earlier second
birth within its fixed search allowance and remains a missing case in both
eight-world denominators.

This is a completed, audited negative experiment. It argues for continuing the
prune-plus-var0-removal platform **while retaining the var1 label-1 constraint**.
It does not establish that all further edits fail, that this genotype is sterile,
or that one particular mechanism explains the losses. The edited families perform
2,845 productive firings and 59 paid births before extinction. Transient funded
reproduction is insufficient for the registered durability criterion.

## Continuation supported by these results

Retain the proven prune-plus-var0-removal genotype with its label1 endpoint
constraint as the platform for further experiments. Do not promote the
fully unconstrained two-label-removal genotype to a durable serial-edit
platform. A useful next prospective test would preserve that output-role anchor
and perturb another independently named feature, with its own fresh panel and
the same founder-specific controls. Alternatively, test the inferred
substrate/resource coupling directly with a fixed mechanistic contrast.

Neither proposal was launched here. The present result establishes a tested
boundary of this edit sequence, not autonomous mutation selection, universal
necessity of label1, indefinite viability, or a fourth successful modification.
