# Selfmod v15 — fresh-seed bounded reproduction replication

Completed 2026-09-27 in the isolated LK workspace. Validated evidence:
`output/selfmod_v15_replication_20260927`. Frozen contract:
`docs/selfmod_v15_replication_preregistration.md`.

**Pruned funded parent replacement passes the combined criterion in 5/8
fresh seeds, below the preregistered 6/8 replication threshold.** The prior
v14 result was 7/8 on reused checkpoints. The panels remain separate; v15
does not replicate that primary claim at the declared threshold. Nevertheless,
all eight fresh pruned families establish, survive and reproduce late under
replacement. Their cultures remain productive and have funded births in every
late window. The three primary failures concern withdrawal-adjusted financing.

## Primary and establishment results

Legacy establishment requires executable, uncensored selected descendants,
productive late windows, income covering rent and all three seed members
remaining alive/productive. Combined success also requires income covering
lineage retirement withdrawals, a funded culture birth in every final
250-step window and a selected-family birth during the final 1,000 steps.
The exact nonnegative financing test remains fixed, including equality passing.

| Policy | Faithful establishment | Prune establishment | Faithful combined | Prune combined |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Native ON | 2/8 | 5/8 | 2/8 | 4/8 |
| OFF | 7/8 | 8/8 | 0/8 | 0/8 |
| Vacancy cap 8 | 3/8 | 8/8 | 0/8 | 0/8 |
| Funded parent replacement, cap 8 | 5/8 | 8/8 | 3/8 | **5/8** |

No arm clears 6/8 combined success. Successful pruned replacement seeds are
131, 137, 149, 163 and 173; faithful replacement succeeds on 137, 149 and 163.
Replacement has two paired combined-success gains for prune (131/173), no
paired losses, and three establishment gains (157/181/191). These are
descriptive paired observations from eight worlds, not a significance claim.

Compared separately with v14, pruned replacement declines from 7/8 to 5/8,
while faithful replacement rises from 1/8 to 3/8. Native pruned ON rises from
0/8 to 4/8. This seed sensitivity limits claims that replacement is uniquely
necessary or that the prior six-seed advantage generalizes unchanged.

## Continuing births and the three financing failures

| Replacement genotype | Subsequent births | Parent retirements | Late selected-family births | Families alive at end | Whole cycles persistent |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Faithful | 734 | 686 | 29 | 5/8 | 6/8 |
| Prune | 1,169 | 1,142 | 170 | 8/8 | 8/8 |

Pruned replacement has 546 culture births during the final 1,000 steps, and
each selected family has 6–32 late births. Every culture reproduces in every
late window. Pruned vacancy8 has only 18 expansion births, zero late culture
births and zero late family births; it blocks 116,994 of 117,012 eligible
opportunities. Its 8/8 establishment cannot be interpreted as continuing
reproduction. OFF similarly preserves establishment without later births.

The failed pruned seeds meet every other requirement. Their late lineage
capital falls 95→76 (157), 83→62 (181), and 98→72 (191), with late minima
48, 48 and 43. Retirement returns leave the lineage but re-enter the free
pool; they are not earned lineage income. The independent stock identity is:

`final − late-start capital = income − rent − retirement returns − starvation returns`.

Late residual starvation returns are zero here. These finite-window capital
declines do not demonstrate extinction; equally, ongoing births and positive
gross income do not erase the frozen financing failures. Funding/retirement
phase can affect endpoints. This 2,000-step assay does not distinguish that
effect from longer-term capital erosion.

Faithful replacement additionally loses selected families on 157/181/191;
157/181 lose the whole cycle. Faithful 131/173 retain establishment and births
but fail financing (−42/−13). Faithful 163 passes at exactly zero surplus.

Creation allowance binds in all eight pruned replacement runs and six
faithful runs. No node/edge ceiling gates occur. Mean productive firings per
live lineage-copy step are 0.951477 for prune and 0.538173 for faithful
replacement; these remain descriptive finite-horizon rates.

## Status and recommended next experiment

Retain bounded replacement as a working funded reproduction mechanism with
strong pruned establishment and continuing-birth evidence. Mark **fresh-seed
combined-financing replication below threshold**, rather than promoting it
to a validated general reproductive platform. Future generations here retain
the initial genotype; this experiment does not test repeated novelty or
open-ended evolution.

The next useful experiment is a separately frozen financing diagnostic on
**all eight v15 seeds**, with unchanged policies and accounting, extended
continuous trajectories and multiple fixed late windows. Compare capital
trends and birth/retirement phases to distinguish endpoint phase from erosion.
Report any additional diagnostic criterion separately from the failed v15
primary verdict. Resolve that issue before a broad mutation campaign or an
engine-default policy change. No further scientific campaign was launched.
