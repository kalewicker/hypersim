## What to continue

The prune platform supports this externally specified reversible transition
while retaining label1. Keep both the broad-var0 twice-edited state and the
restored prune state as finite-horizon viable platforms. The next distinct
question is maintenance across a longer prospectively fixed horizon, or another
named role-preserving structural change with founder-specific controls. Repeated
success at the same third reversion would add replication but little new scope.

Neither extension was launched here. This assay contains three introduced
changes, with the third reversing the second; it does not demonstrate three
novel or beneficial genotypes, autonomous selection, indefinitely stable
reproduction or training admission.
