# Selfmod v18 — named variants under funded parent replacement

The reference R1 RHS-edge prune meets the unchanged v17 durability criterion
in **8/8 fresh worlds**, exceeding the fixed 6/8 threshold. Faithful inheritance
passes **2/8**. R1 LHS-edge removal and an extra RHS-created node each pass
**0/8**; their selected families are extinct in every world by the final step.
Neither alternative qualifies for continuation. The prune remains the supported
platform for the next controlled self-modification experiment.

This is a new prospective comparison, with candidates chosen from completed
v11 evidence and criteria fixed before new output. All three named variants
had passed v11's eight short-horizon functional worlds. Those earlier passes
do not establish durable funded reproduction. The new result supports one
specific variant, operating point and finite horizon; it does not establish
indefinite viability, useful novelty, repeated self-modification, or a universal
ordering of mutation operators.

## Frozen design

The contracts are [the preregistration](selfmod_v18_variants_preregistration.md)
and [exact candidate identities](selfmod_v18_variants_contract.json). The fixed
new seeds were **263, 269, 271, 277, 281, 283, 293 and 307**, with no overlap
in prior selfmod seed/world-seed manifests. No seed was replaced or removed.

Each world shared a native faithful prefix ending at the first naturally funded
R1 birth. The trigger steps were 22, 14, 19, 18, 18, 14, 37 and 28. Initial
post-birth populations were five to eight; all four treatments were admissible
without a resident cull. The same paying parent, child identity and complete
birth phase were used for all four forks:

| Treatment | Fixed identity | Change to the selected R1 child |
|---|---|---|
| Faithful | Native faithful inheritance | Original R1 program |
| Reference prune | `op11_member0_draw1` | Remove RHS edge from var0 to the created node; retain the other RHS edge |
| LHS-edge removal | `op07_member0_draw0` | Remove the required LHS edge; retain labels and both RHS edges |
| Extra RHS-created node | `op08_member0_draw0` | Add a second created node with label 1; retain the original edges |

Exact recorded operators/parameters/specs/class hashes were authenticated
against the v11 audit. The normal threshold 100 / transfer 50, sibling births,
native RNG draws, inherited creation allowance and refill were retained.
The one named edit was applied before child genealogy registration and consumed
no extra scheduler RNG. All later births inherit their genotype faithfully.
These are separately paired alternatives in the same resident culture, not
simultaneous coexistence of competing introduced genotypes.

Each treatment continued for 10,000 steps under the unchanged cap-eight
funded parent-replacement policy. All 32 trajectories completed; none was
missing or enumeration-censored. Total science was **320,170 advancing steps**:
320,000 continuations and 170 shared prefix steps. The v17 physical configuration
was retained with only world seed changed. No subsidy, top-up, extra allowance,
reset or scientific retry was introduced.

The primary criterion directly reuses v17's unchanged implementation: 2,000
warmup steps followed by eight 1,000-step windows, each four 250-step quarters.
It requires family and all culture members at every scored boundary, aggregate
family capital covering the next scheduled rent, productive family and members
every quarter, actual culture funded births every quarter and actual family
funded births every 1,000 steps. It requires an executable uncancelled child,
a full uncensored horizon and exact native accounting. It does not require
nonnegative endpoint capital change or promise per-individual rent coverage.

## Material costs and endpoint sensitivity

Native receipts show this allowance cost per productive family firing across
the complete 10,000-step trajectories:

| Treatment | Productive family firings | Creation allowance spent | Allowance units per productive firing |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Faithful | 31,594 | 63,188 | 2 |
| Prune | 120,210 | 120,210 | 1 |
| LHS-edge removal | 5,374 | 16,122 | 3 |
| Extra RHS-created node | 2,126 | 6,378 | 3 |

These are **creation-allowance units, not capital tokens or birth payments**.
Removing a matching constraint does not necessarily reduce material cost:
the LHS-edge-removal variant retains both emitted edges and has greater native
creation spend per productive firing. The extra-node variant adds material.
The observed cost ordering and early family losses motivate a resource-mechanism
diagnostic, but this comparison does not isolate cost as the sole causal factor.
Changed matching, graph structure, sibling competition and canonical resolver
ordering can all affect the outcomes.

During scored steps, extra-node cultures reach up to 514 world nodes, whereas
the pruned cultures peak at 183. Existing node ceilings are native pre-phase
growth gates, so a completed boundary above 500 does not mean the gate was
disabled. No trajectory reaches the hard 20,000-node stop. Whole-culture
creation-blocked root-steps remain common even in the passing prune arm
(58,752 scored root-steps); a durable pass is not evidence of unconstrained
growth or absence of resource gates. Scored LHS-edge-removal cultures also
record 480 edge-ceiling-blocked root-steps. Gate counts include all resident
roots and cannot be attributed solely to the selected family.

The old v15 combined endpoint function was retained unchanged. Counts for the
five disjoint 2,000-step segments are:

| Treatment | 1–2000 | 2001–4000 | 4001–6000 | 6001–8000 | 8001–10000 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Faithful | 4/8 | 1/8 | 2/8 | 1/8 | 2/8 |
| Prune | 5/8 | 4/8 | 5/8 | 5/8 | 4/8 |
| LHS-edge removal | 1/8 | 0/8 | 0/8 | 0/8 | 0/8 |
| Extra RHS-created node | 0/8 | 0/8 | 0/8 | 0/8 | 0/8 |

Every pruned seed has both positive and negative scored-window stock changes,
including all retirement withdrawals. All maintain the prospectively declared
rent coverage and recurring paid births. The old endpoint result and the
durability result remain different measurements. Historical v15 remains 5/8;
v17 remains 8/8 under its own fixed criterion. No old or serial panels are pooled.

## Validation, provenance and reproduction

- **36 selfmod library tests passed**, including a new native named-birth test
  for genotype identity, payment, allowance, siblings, RNG and exact default
  prune material. All six unchanged durability test methods passed.
- Engineering seeds 7/42 covered all eight treatment arms: 2,000 uninterrupted
  steps exactly matched two separately restored 1,000-step processes, including
  concatenated rows, intermediate/final states and allocator sidecars.
  Faithful/prune initialization, prefix, full paid fork and both initial states
  also matched the frozen v17 binary. **32,122 advancing engineering steps**
  include 32,000 continuations and two 61-step prefix sets; excluded from science.
- The full independent audit reconstructs prefixes, first triggers, all four
  paired paid births, every continuation receipt, checkpoints and verdicts.
  Every window reconciles `end − start = income − rent − retirement returns
  − starvation returns`. It rejects ten corruption classes, including wrong
  candidate spec/class/selection, rent, RNG, cap, payment, donor, trigger parent
  and false replication after seed removal.
- Five additional mocked engineering views reject false untriggered markers,
  removed admissible branches, a shrunken denominator, swapped candidate births
  and altered candidate operators. Untampered evidence passes.
- Fresh extractions of both engineering and scientific source archives compile
  offline/locked with `cargo check -p hg-selfmod --bin selfmod-variants`.
- Exact verification uses first eligible seed 263, regardless of outcome. Fresh
  initialization, prefix and all four paid births match exactly; all four
  separate 10,000-step replays match rows, ten checkpoints, final states and
  allocator sidecars: **116 exact receipts**, **40,022 advancing steps**, excluded
  from science. The figure was visually inspected against audited counts.

## What to continue

Continue the **R1 RHS-edge prune platform**. Stop promoting these two failed
alternatives on the basis of short-horizon function. Their archives remain
useful negative comparisons; do not silently retry with altered resources.

The next self-modification assay should introduce **one additional named edit
at a later naturally funded birth within an established prune family**, paired
with faithful second-birth inheritance. Fix the second edit, readiness rule,
fresh seeds and durability horizon beforehand; retain native payment, sibling
competition, exact allocator forks and stock-flow accounting. That would test
one further self-modification event on the demonstrated platform. Current
evidence only covers one initial edit and faithful subsequent reproduction.
A separately preregistered creation-budget diagnostic could test the cost
mechanism if needed; it would be a changed operating point, not a rescue of
these failed results. No second-edit assay, broad campaign or training run is
launched here.
