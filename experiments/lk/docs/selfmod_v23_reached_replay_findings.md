The focal family is the actual paid third child and its immutable genealogical descendants. Older same-genotype prune relatives are excluded. Both faithful families consisted of one live descendant throughout their final 1,000-step intervals; both lost that final child with zero capital and creation allowance 200. Thus allowance debt is not the explanation for these two losses, even though debt can occur elsewhere on this platform.
