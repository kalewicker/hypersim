## Interpretation and next step

The fresh panel reproduces the registered long-maintenance qualification for
both label1-retaining states. It also repeats one faithful focal-family loss
against maintained restoration in a different world, while exposing a distinct
entry-cap limitation in seed503. These observations support continuing the
prune platform under the tested operating point and finite horizon. They do
not establish an unrestricted self-modification process or a comparative
fitness ranking.

The next useful step is an explicitly specified exact replay of a reached
50,000-step pair, followed by a read-only financing analysis of seed541 around
the rent-floor crossing and comparison with V22 seed463. That would strengthen
verification and test whether the repeated loss has a shared mechanism before
introducing a fourth edit. No further native assay, mutation or training is
launched by this report.
