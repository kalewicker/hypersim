# Selfmod v17: fresh prospective durability test

The named R1 RHS-edge prune passes the new funded-reproduction durability
criterion in **8/8 fresh worlds under cap-eight parent replacement**, exceeding
the preregistered 6/8 threshold. Faithful replacement passes in 3/8. The five
paired gains are seeds 227, 233, 239, 241 and 251, with no paired losses.
This establishes finite-horizon durability for this variant and operating
point. It does not revise v15's failed 5/8 combined-financing result, establish
indefinite viability, or demonstrate repeated self-modification or novelty.

## What was fixed before the fresh panel

The [preregistration](selfmod_v17_durability_preregistration.md) was frozen
before implementation and scientific execution. The eight new seeds were
211, 223, 227, 229, 233, 239, 241 and 251; archived prior manifest seed lists
show no overlap. Every seed remains in the denominator. All eight seeds
triggered the first naturally funded R1 birth within 35 steps, with initial
post-birth populations of five to seven; no seed replacement or resident cull
was needed.

The ordinary v15 configuration was retained, with the physical scoring horizon
set to 10,000. Each seed shared one native faithful prefix and a complete paid
birth fork. The fork compared the exact v11 variant `op11_member0_draw1` with
faithful inheritance, preserving siblings, payment, allowances and RNG state.
Future births inherit their genotype faithfully. Each genotype then received
ON, OFF, vacancy8 and replace8 continuations, giving 64 new trajectories of
10,000 steps each. The total scientific work was **640,165 advancing steps**:
640,000 continuations and 165 shared prefix steps. All trajectories completed;
none was missing or enumeration-censored.

Replacement retains the normal threshold of 100 and transfer of 50 tokens.
At capacity, a parent completes its paid birth and only that paying parent
retires; its remaining capital returns to the free pool. Vacancy treatment
permits paid births only when room exists. The cap is eight at completed
boundaries. No subsidy, top-up, or additional creation allowance was introduced.

The new criterion scores eight disjoint 1,000-step windows after a fixed
2,000-step warmup. It requires continuous family and three-member culture
presence at all scored phase boundaries, productive family and members every
250 steps, a funded culture birth every 250 steps, and a funded selected-family
birth every 1,000 steps. At every scored boundary, aggregate family capital
must cover `rent × living family copies`. This floor derives from the next
scheduled rent obligation; it was not fitted to a historical stock minimum.
It does not guarantee that each individual can pay or that any longer horizon
will survive. All birth, retirement, money, allowance, cap and genealogy
receipts must reconcile.

The new criterion has no nonnegative endpoint-change requirement. Retirement
withdrawals remain fully counted in the exact stock-flow identity. The old
v15 establishment and combined-financing functions remain unchanged as
secondary metrics on five separate 2,000-step segments. Their local scoring
views never alter the physical configuration.

Every pruned replacement seed has both positive and negative scored-window
stock changes after retirement withdrawals. Nevertheless, all maintain the
declared rent coverage, living culture and recurring paid births. The old
endpoint criterion fluctuates from 3/8 to 5/8 across this fresh panel, while
the new durability result remains 8/8. These are different prospectively
declared questions; the new pass does not rescue the earlier endpoint failure.

Faithful replacement passes for 211, 223 and 229. Seed 227 maintains the family
and culture but has no family birth during steps 6001–7000. Seeds 233, 239 and
251 lose the family and culture. Seed 241 retains a family copy but loses R2
and R3, with birth gaps. The faithful arm's 138 scored family births and 1,869
scored culture births therefore do not imply durability in every world.

## Validation and evidence

The independent auditor reconstructs every native prefix, trigger, paired
paid birth, continuation receipt, phase stock, window and verdict. Each window
reconciles `end − start = income − rent − retirement returns − starvation
returns`. Native matching, effect classification, hashing, ChaCha and allocator
reuse are checked through native receipts and exact replays; they are not
independently reimplemented.

Exact allocator sidecars are recorded from fresh initialization onward. The
faithful restored birth is checked against the original native in-memory birth.
The missing historical allocator ordering discovered in v16 therefore does
not limit this new panel.

Validation completed:

- 35 LK selfmod library tests and six criterion test methods passed, including
  negative endpoint change with sustained paid births, rent-floor crossing,
  extinction, productivity/birth gaps, censoring, accounting corruption and
  false verdict rejection.
- Engineering seeds 7 and 42 covered all 16 arms: 2,000 uninterrupted steps
  matched two separately restored 1,000-step processes, with exact concatenated
  rows, intermediate/final states and allocator sidecars. These **64,061
  advancing steps** are excluded from science.
- The full audit rejected seven corruption classes: rent, RNG, cap, birth
  payment, retirement donor, fresh trigger parent and false replication after
  seed removal. Three additional mocked missing-case checks rejected false
  untriggered markers, removed admissible branches and a shrunken denominator.
- A fresh extraction of the archived source compiled with
  `cargo check --offline --locked -p hg-selfmod --bin selfmod-durability`.
- The first eligible seed in the fixed order, 211, was regenerated from fresh
  initialization. Its prefix and both paid births matched exactly. All four
  bounded 10,000-step arms then replayed exactly, including ten checkpoints
  and allocator sidecars: **110 exact receipts**, **40,014 advancing steps**,
  excluded from science.
- The comparison PNG was visually inspected; its counts match audited data.

An initial engineering attempt failed during historical reference validation,
before any simulation, because it looked for the nested v15 audit hash in
v16's raw evidence map. The corrected lookup uses v16's authenticated input
provenance. The failed directory was retained separately; the successful
`selfmod_v17_durability_preflight_v2_20260927` passed before the scientific
archive was frozen. No scientific source repair or panel retry occurred.

Durable authority is
`output/selfmod_v17_durability_20260927/manifest.json`, `status.json` and
`audit.json`. The folder also contains the frozen source archive/binary,
preregistration, seed inventory, authenticated v11/v15/v16 references, raw
per-seed receipts, checkpoints, independent audit, exact replay, test/archive
verification, post-result interpretation and plot provenance. Original
historical evidence and imported LK provenance were preserved.

## Implementation and what to continue

The physical financing kernel remains the v16 implementation. The new
`selfmod-durability` binary and `birth_durability.rs` add fresh-prefix selection
and complete allocator-preserving forks. `run_selfmod_durability.py` freezes
the instrument and executes the fixed panel; `audit_selfmod_durability.py`
reconstructs the evidence and scores the preregistered criterion. Separate
criterion, exact-replay and missing-case checks validate the new contract.
`summarize_selfmod_durability.py` supplies descriptive post-result analysis.
The freshness guard prevents treating a rerun of this already-used panel as
another fresh scientific replication. The frozen binary can replay receipts
into new exclusive directories.

The supported next experiment is a **small preregistered comparison of named
competing variants in the funded cap-eight replacement setting**. Keep this
durability criterion, native paid-birth accounting and exact paired forks;
fix candidates, fresh seeds and advancement rules before output. This would
test whether the platform distinguishes variants beyond faithful versus this
one prune. It is preferable to another extension of the same worlds or an
open-ended mutation campaign. No such campaign or training admission is
launched by this result.
