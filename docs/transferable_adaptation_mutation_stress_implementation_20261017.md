## Proposal law and provenance

The new kernel uses the original3/4 mutation gate. It enumerates every changed
single-source substitution, ordered by instruction ID then new source, and retains
only those passing D0's syntax validator. There is no reachability, construction,
performance or reproductive filtering and no protected useful gene. Selection
is uniform over the valid set. The original kernel and its probabilities remain
unchanged. An empty grammar neighborhood yields an explicit copy, with no second
random draw. Host grammar checking is declared scaffolding rather than native
organism computation.

For this five-instruction family, the legal neighborhood contains14 candidates,
including materially unreachable edits. Identity has probability1/4 and each
candidate3/56. Canonical set hashes, counts, chosen indices, gate outcomes and
exact probability fractions are exported for every grammar request. Native
receipts retain all raw random draws. Certificates are reconstructed after the
run and cannot influence the proposals. Independent Python replay regenerates
the complete proposal law and validates the certificates against every request.

The appended kernel enum variant preserves all five old discriminants. The new
stress profile has its own streaming identity, policy and codec `HGEMMS01`.
Old live checkpoint payload layouts and default profiles retain their meanings.
Frozen-transfer profiles reject the grammar kernel. All native accounting,
development, death, copying and temporal rules remain unchanged.

## Retention screen

The screen follows behavior across changed genotypes. A qualifying root completes
paid development, attempts at least four paid actions and succeeds on at least
three quarters of its attempts. Each founder family needs a path of three paid
parent-child edges whose members all qualify. Standardized founders are generation0.
Every child edge must reconcile the actual paid request, copying charge, native
B24 endowment and child genotype. A mutation need not preserve genotype identity
to retain useful behavior.

Each useful arm must also exceed its matched ancestor by at least one active
completed offspring and eight units of whole-world net resource gain. Grammar
must pass in all four world/pattern cells, with faithful controls passing too.
Original SourceKernel is a predeclared comparator, not a kernel chosen after
results. Invalid, unreachable, inactive, no-request and censored outcomes remain
in their original denominators. A failed faithful or grammar gate stops this
version before discovery or held-out exposure.
