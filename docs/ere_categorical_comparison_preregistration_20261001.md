## Design and why it differs

See the [prospective contract](ere_categorical_comparison_v1.md) and
[machine-readable design](../configs/ere_categorical_comparison_v1.json).

The previous direction assay failed its frozen usefulness/alignment criteria.
Its follow-up mapping diagnosed rare reward-changing shared-RNG switches; the
direct categorical adapter subsequently passed arithmetic engineering checks,
but has not established native improvement. This new design retains the native
world, grammar, utility, ES implementation and raw categorical estimator. It
changes only the prospective comparison intervention and validation estimator:

- Independently sampled A/B estimates for both methods; no shared ES noise basis.
- The same rollout budget per estimate, with explicit differences in antithetic
  seed coupling and number of distinct native samples.
- Group-centered directions, both applied at fixed L2 radius .1, rather than
  comparing different raw update magnitudes. This is external caller arithmetic,
  not a change to either optimizer or the adapter API.
- Shared fresh base-only native validation; full legal-leaf probability ratios
  estimate expected utility differences with a fixed control variate. No
  proposed-arm native measurements, weight clipping, survivor filtering or
  self-normalization. This does not guarantee lower variance.
- Eight fixed paired estimator blocks. Support requires categorical independent
  alignment, positive utility effect and positive categorical-minus-ES effect,
  each passing its one-sided sign test, plus positive effect in both contexts
  and an aggregate weighted-validity point guard. No result is available yet.

Full science contains 4,096 estimation and 8,192 base-only validation evaluations,
