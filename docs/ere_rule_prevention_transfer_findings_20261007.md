Completed 2026-10-07. Panel `E2-rule-prevention-transfer-panel-v1`; native

[prospective contract](ere_rule_prevention_transfer_v1.md) and configuration

## Outcome

The fixed 72-cell, nine-world panel completes all 6,912 native advances and
72 paid non-advancing Seal initializations, with zero optimizer updates.
Independent accounting and all ten prospective transfer criteria pass.
Learned rule creation plus paid prevention preserves the specified useful
class continuously in **9/9 worlds**, versus **1/9 same-theta masked** and
**2/9 trained-blind** funded controls. The explicit-correct-action capacity
control passes 9/9. Resource exhaustion, unpaid, disabled and no-hazard
controls behave exactly as registered. Frozen archive accounting and all
five auditor test groups reproduce without live-source fallback or native work.

This resolves transfer of the frozen encoder's rule-creation behavior into
the paid-prevention environment. It does not demonstrate learned defense:
the interceptor, its priority and its finite fuel budget remain authored.
The encoder selects the two birth edits, not whether or when to intercept.
It has no maintenance feedback or optimizer update in this panel. The same
interceptor can preserve a useless body, as the masked/blind failures show.

All scientific acceptance, sustained-claim, full training-admission and
independent-physics-replay flags remain false. These are existing exposed
worlds, one existing native seed, a finite horizon, scripted requests and
preloaded resources—not unseen-world generalization or ecological autonomy.

## First lifetimes and evidence limits

All nine learned-paid and oracle worlds birth at 5 and install at 6; target
ordinary firing starts at 7. Their genotype/installed first spells remain
continuous through 96, with right-censored lower bounds 91/90 and 45 target
fires per world. The learned no-hazard arm has the same target continuity
without requests or defense payments. Exhaustion loses the class at 48,
giving exact first lifetimes 43/42 and 21 target fires. Unpaid/disabled lose
it at 16, giving 11/10 and five target fires. Root survival is censored at 96
throughout and is not credited as rule survival.

The unchanged survival instrument keeps funded point half-lives null and
point-estimate ratio flags false. The separately preregistered bounded
continuity metric passes; censor bounds are not relabeled observed deaths.
Exhaustion's nine-member exact half-lives 43/42 exceed 16 and pass its numerical
ratio flags, without sustained/scientific admission. Unpaid/disabled fail.
Actual family-touch mean interval is 12.6 for learned exposed arms; no-hazard
has mean 4. External request/clock activity and initialization do not inflate
family-touch counts or replace the independent 16-step comparator.

At every exposed resolution, native matching enumerates the damage binding
even when paid interception wins. The independent audit verifies the losing
candidate against the same live payload and pre-step graph as the absorber.
Native conflict arbitration/payment, not blanket META invisibility, prevents
the lesion. Defense/threat nodes remain isolated; no original-body restore
cache, foreign family edges or funded repair are introduced. Initial classes
are excluded from novelty, and firing attribution uses actual pre-step tables
and three distinct ordinary nodes.

The primitive's positive capacity evidence, the older repair panel and its
negative first-lifetime result remain unchanged. This is not a fresh matched
repair comparison; staging differs across versions. See
[primitive findings](ere_rule_prevention_primitive_findings_20261007.md) and
[prior maintenance findings](ere_rule_maintenance_findings_20261007.md).

## Recommended next boundary

Do not repeat encoder fitting or simply preload more fuel: the useful-rule
creation and finite prevention capacity questions now have positive evidence.
The next informative boundary is a small **policy-controlled defense-allocation
diagnostic with a real finite-resource opportunity cost**. Make spending on
defense compete with another valuable graph-resident use; keep never-defend and
always-defend controls. First verify that the task rewards different choices
under different observable threat/resource states; only then preregister a
bounded learning comparison. This would test whether a controller learns when
to maintain its rule, instead of relying on a universally enabled seeded
interceptor. Resource replenishment and longer ecological trials remain separate
questions. None of those follow-up steps is launched by this completed panel.
