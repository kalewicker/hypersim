## Classification and gate

Precedence is Rejected, Unmeasured, Neutral by executable RCF bytes,
NearNeutral by exact typed world trajectory plus projected hashes and accepted
rewrite counts, Catastrophic, then Divergent. Catastrophe is the union of a
50-contiguous-zero-rewrite freeze and strictly greater-than-fourfold world
growth; neither overrides Neutral/NearNeutral precedence. Root absence is
rejection, not a neutral/quarantine success. Unmeasured remains in denominators.

The frozen JSON is the authoritative threshold inventory. Require 10,000
primary draws; validity >=40%, NearNeutral >=10% in the prescribed overall,
structural, active structural, topology, active topology, actual-pattern-change
and active-pattern-change strata; Catastrophic <=25% where prescribed.
Require >=2,000 active structural and active topology samples,
>=1,000 activation samples, >=1,000 actual pattern changes,
>=500 active actual pattern changes, >=100 actual mode changes and
>=20 trajectory-changing mode edits. Require all operators/roles/states,
both at-cap and off-cap populations, 50 Neutral soundness checks,
zero soundness failures and zero Unmeasured, plus all complete parent baselines.

Compute native and independent summaries and require exact agreement.
A full gate pass requires complete measurement and every numerical predicate,
successful controls/full audits and final seal. Training remains false even
after a gate pass: optimizer distribution/semantic-effect/signal-noise
prechecks and separate training admission/authorization remain pending.
