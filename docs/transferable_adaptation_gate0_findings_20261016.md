# TA-1 Gate 0: authored useful inheritance passes

Date: 2026-10-16. Development-only positive-control result.

The fixed [Gate0 contract](../experiments/evolving_minds/contracts/ta1_gate0_v1.md)
passed on both predeclared exposed worlds. Independent physical replay passed
for every segment and publication. The authored useful edit supports paid,
functionally active descendants in this funded profile. Held-out environmental
transfer, stochastic discovery, and cumulative reuse remain untested.

## Results and accounting

Both arms began with identical Delayed founders. At paid founder reservations,
Useful installed the known g103 source2-to-0 edit into children; Ancestor copied
the original program. All subsequent reproduction used faithful copying.
There was no candidate search or outcome-based replacement.

| 17 | 10 | 2 | 63.25 | -128.00 | +191.25 |

Active offspring completed paid development and attempted at least one paid
action. The stricter inheritance predicate required successful work by every
member of the witness path. Both families in each world reached four subsequent
paid faithful transmissions, exceeding the fixed three-transmission requirement.
Installation of the edit into the first child was excluded from that count.

The world17 witness paths were `44→139→236→331→434` and
`48→145→242→337→432`; world23 paths were `44→139→236→331→428` and
`46→143→240→335→430`. Each edge was reconciled to native settled request,
copying payment, B24 child endowment and unchanged useful genotype. No sibling
aggregation or mere maximum ancestry depth supplied this result.

Net resource gain is total world harvest minus native dissipation, with every
founder and descendant included. Useful harvest/dissipation were 928/864.75 and
960/895.75; Ancestor values were 64/192 and 96/223. Endowments and refunds were
treated as transfers, not additional harvest. These are finite-window accounts,
not claims of indefinite resource sustainability.

Useful and Ancestor were compared in matched separate worlds. This establishes
an offspring-production advantage in this assay; it does not measure competitive
invasion in a mixed population. Two exposed worlds are engineering controls,
not a powered generalization panel. The historical cumulative campaign and its
negative result retain their original interpretation.

## Verification and next boundary

All nine new Python tests passed, including live-fixture checks that reject an
omitted publication, a forged intermediate graph and a free-copy proposal.
Synthetic endpoint tests reject installation-as-inheritance, sibling aggregation,
attempt-only functional paths, missing paid edges and resource misaccounting.

All12 targeted Rust tests passed: two new TA tests, four funded-admission tests,
three handoff tests and three retained-lifecycle tests. They include native/live
continuation, worker/allocation invariance, profile binding and corrupt checkpoint
refusal. Across the new and available legacy Python cases,22 tests passed.
The [validation receipt](transferable_adaptation_gate0_validation_20261016.json)
records counts, unavailable suites and evidence identities. Frozen sources and
the native binary hash were also rechecked against the working checkout.

Legacy Python handoff/descendant decision tests passed. Their historical replay
cases were skipped because the archived fixtures are absent locally. Separate
legacy streaming and funded-admission Python suites could not initialize without
their `EVOLVING_MINDS_*_FIXTURE` environment variables; those suites were not
reported as passing. The new fixture's full independent replay and tamper tests
provide current native evidence without restoring the large historical archives.
