## What the program can modify

The editor node owns two additional integer properties: enabled (0 or 1) and
an operator mask (0 through 31). The existing editor-mode field stays zero.
Native local rules bind the wallet, editor and, where needed, the targeted gene.
The graph's current policy guards endogenous admission. Each successful edit
uses the preserved native matcher and apply path, spends two wallet units and
records its exact before/native-after/settled states and invocation provenance.

| Family | Native modification | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| Duplicate | Copy an executable gene, jitter its stage by -1, 0 or +1, assign a fresh semantic serial | Eight genes; next serial below 32 |
| Retune | Jitter an executable gene's stage in place | Source stage 0 through 3 |
| Delete | Remove the selected gene and its ownership edge | At least one gene remains |
| Toggle enabled | Change the editor's enabled bit | Only the opposite boolean value |
| Set mask | Change its operator permissions | All 32 mask values, including the unchanged value |

This is a fixed, finite variation kernel over graph-resident reaction genes and
editor policy. It does not modify the engine, physical law, mutation kernel,
settlement schedule or scientific acceptance criteria. Nonexecutable stages -1
and 4 remain in the program and pay storage/copy costs; duplicate and retune
cannot target them. Deletion leaves serial holes. Serials are never reused and
the complete serial mapping and next-serial counter are copied at birth.

Every admitted family and target is enumerated. Selection draws a family
uniformly, then a target uniformly within that family using the persisted
ChaCha8 stream, without utility or assay outcomes. This prevents the mask
family's 32 targets from receiving 32 times a single-target family's selection
weight. Reversing enumeration and offsetting initial allocation by seven
produce matching program, energy and RNG-cursor projections. Exceeding a bound
rejects the operation rather than truncating its candidate list.

## Costs, controls and refusals

The new local diagnostic compartments reuse the original native chemistry and
integer economy: parcel energy 16, settlement 0/4/8/14/16, one unit per reaction,
storage one plus current gene count, edit cost two and copy cost three plus
current parent gene count. A birth transfers eight existing wallet units and
leaves the parent's reserve of 24; children receive zero reservoir or environment.
Each case has a fixed finite bank. Death, exhaustion, insufficient funding,
disabled/masked policy, last-gene deletion, gene/serial caps and nonexecutable
mutation targets are retained through their full two-tick horizons.

The frozen arm never edits. The external arm gets one blind paid native edit
after birth; it bypasses endogenous enable/mask permissions while retaining
native funding and actual decoded-policy guards. The nonheritable arm resets
the complete founder program, including serials and editor fields, while paying
the current parent's copy charge. The heritable arm transmits a disabled policy
and its child refuses endogenous editing; the matched nonheritable control
transmits the enabled founder and permits an edit. These are functional control
checks, not comparative evolutionary results.

The causal diagnostic begins with the same [0] founder, enabled editor, all
operators allowed, capital 40 and no reservoir. A paid native disable blocks
the next duplication attempt. A paid neutral mask edit permits duplication.
A private fixture-only paid native restoration permits duplication again.
Restoration is explicitly authored, outside the organism's invocation route,
and spends an additional two units. It establishes reversible permission
dependence; it is neither autonomous recovery nor a perfectly matched wallet-cost
comparison. Disabling endogenous editing can be an absorbing state under this
kernel. A policy that masks its own policy-changing operations can also strand
that route. These consequences are not repaired during organism execution.

The authored continuation processes one declared resource tick, deletes serial
1 from [0,1,2], changes the mask to 17 and duplicates serial 0. Its program has
serials [0,2,3] and next serial 4. Original and restored execution perform the
same paid birth and two continuation ticks, including reversed candidates,
with exact states, child programs and receipts. No setup probe is counted as
endogenous discovery.
