# V9 / N3 — scope discipline: what the metabolic re-run cannot mean

**Written and committed BEFORE Phase 2 is scored**, per the V9 plan's instruction to state this
first rather than after. Its purpose is to fix in advance the boundary of what a metabolic positive
can license, so a strong Phase-2 result cannot be quietly over-read.

## The settled fact this rests on

Selection on this substrate is **settled degenerate-only at power**. M4.4 ran 30 established seeds
over 15 000 steps against a matched, verified-clean drift null (the two genome traits read by no
rule; sign balance 43%/53%, envelope RMS 6.33):

- **`satiation`** (the trade-off trait) is **drift-dominated**: magnitude ratio 1.01, sign fraction
  0.50, sign-test p = 1.000.
- **`replication_threshold`** shows a real, strong directional bias (97% down, p < 0.001) whose
  **magnitude sits inside the drift envelope** (ratio 1.16). It is the *free* trait — node-funded
  replication means nothing opposes it sliding to its floor — so even its clean sign bias is the
  pre-registered degenerate route, not selection resolving a trade-off.

This is not a null result awaiting more power. It **is** the powered result.

## Therefore

**The system has metabolism but no meaningful evolution.**

1. **A metabolic boundary is a legitimate object of study**, and Phase 2 can establish one. If the
   metabolic channel detects a carrier/substrate boundary, that is a real finding about a system with
   energy flow, birth, death, and persistence.

2. **An evolutionary boundary is not available on this substrate**, and **no metabolic result can
   supply it.** A strong metabolic signal says the carrier class is dynamically distinguishable from
   substrate; it says nothing about heritable variation under selection, because the trait dynamics
   have been measured at power and are drift-dominated. Any sentence of the form "the metabolic
   channel shows evolution / adaptation / fitness differences" is forbidden output for this track.

3. **The tri-ecology gate's premise is now contradicted, not merely unmet.** That gate presupposes
   selection which this substrate has been shown **at power** not to exhibit. It is therefore not
   "waiting downstream" pending more work on the current line — its precondition has been tested and
   failed. It should be **re-examined on its own terms** (does it need a different substrate? a
   parent-side replication cost that reopens the node-funded decision? a different criterion?). That
   re-examination is its own decision, outside this track, but **it must not be deferred silently**,
   and no result in this track should be presented as progress toward it.

## Relationship to the shadow result

The M3 retention finding (structural coarse-graining retains ~0% of the metabolic channel's
information) makes the metabolic channel the more informative one, but it does **not** upgrade what
the system contains. A channel that carries more of the causal work still carries metabolism, not
evolution. Re-scoring the ladder changes *which channel we measure the boundary on*; it does not
change *what the biology does*.

## Forbidden (restating the plan's list for this file's scope)

- Treating any metabolic positive as an evolutionary result.
- Proceeding to, or planning toward, the tri-ecology gate on the assumption its premise still holds.
- Presenting the ~0% retention as causal independence between channels — the channels are causally
  coupled by construction (energy crossing zero *causes* death, a structural event); the ~0% is
  **class-level information retention**, and strong individual-level coupling washes out because WL
  classes pool carriers at different energies.
