# Complete coherent isolated resource pilot: findings

Date: 2026-09-28. Profile `E2-complete-coherent-resource-pilot-v1`.

## Outcome and decision

The single authorized pilot completed and its frozen independent audit passed.
All 168 parent baselines and 55 measured children completed 100 steps:
22,300 attempted and advancing Engine steps; no matching/store cap hits,
no Unmeasured draws, and 22 Neutral soundness checks with zero failures.
All 5,376 damage controls and 27 tamper categories passed.

The long horizon matters: ten draws classified Divergent at eight steps are now
Catastrophic because they develop a contiguous 50-step no-rewrite window.
NearNeutral and Neutral totals are unchanged. These are not ten new identity
failures, nor ten growth explosions.

Resource readiness for the full trial is **not established**. Native execution
under the actual first-50-Neutral policy extrapolates to about 2,631 seconds,
but the frozen mutation/child replay phase alone projects well beyond the
unchanged 900-second shared auditor cap under the scenarios below.
Optimize/profile that replay before proposing another fresh resource pilot.
Do not launch the full trial with the expectation that this receipt proves it
will fit. Scientific and training admission remain closed.

## Transparent full-trial projections — not confidence intervals

Let `D=10000/64=156.25`. Fixed native overhead is
`277.110 - 160.923 - 25.940 = 90.248 s`; fixed shared auditor overhead is
`160.923 - 22.668 = 138.255 s`.
These are measured phase partitions, not a guarantee of linear scaling.

| Scenario | Projected cost | Important assumption |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Native, actual first-50-Neutral policy | 2631.077 s (43.85 min) | Scale non-trajectory work and 33 RCF-changing traces per 64 draws; add only 50 Neutral checks |
| Native, repeat the pilot's complete trace mix | 4143.360 s (69.06 min) | Repeats all 22 Neutral checks per 64, unlike the actual full policy |
| Native, all 10,000 draws need a mean-cost child | 4774.060 s (79.57 min) | Mean observed child cost, not a maximum-cost upper bound |
| Shared auditor, trace-count-weighted heuristic | 2284.005 s (38.07 min) | Treat mutation/child phase as entirely trace-proportional; ~5,206 children under sampled nonneutral rate plus 50 checks |
| Shared auditor, repeat observed per-draw replay cost | 3680.159 s (61.34 min) | Scale complete mutation/child phase by D |
| Output, sampled actual trace policy | 237.87 MiB | Fixed files plus scaled draw/classification rows and ~5,206 children |
| Output, 10,000 mean-sized children | 322.30 MiB | Same row costs; every draw has a child |

Native policy formula:
`90.248 + D*(1.273 + 14.846) + 50*(9.821/22)`.
Auditor scenarios:
`138.255 + 22.668*(5206.25/55)` and
`138.255 + 22.668*D`.
The auditor does not separately time edit replay versus child replay in this
version. Its trace-weighted scenario is **not a proven lower bound**: Neutral
and RCF-changing trace costs differ, and decoding/edit overhead must still run
for every draw. The repeat-mix scenario is likewise not a proven upper bound.
The 64-draw prefix is not a representative-size cost study; no population
uncertainty or full-run success probability is estimated.

Both auditor heuristics exceed 900 seconds substantially. Combined with the
policy-adjusted native estimate, total wall projects to roughly 82–105 minutes,
not within the unchanged 60-minute full envelope. Output scenarios are below
4 GiB, but compression ratios and expensive mutation/shape mixtures can change.
The 100-step parent baseline footprint is measured; full-trial RSS is not
validated. No cap was raised and none of these calculations authorize a run.

a new prospective profile for any changed checker/producer and obtain approval
for a fresh resource pilot before asserting cost improvement.

to fit the cap.
