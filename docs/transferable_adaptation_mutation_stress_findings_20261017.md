# TA-1 mutation-stress findings

Date: 2026-10-17. Status: implementation and independent audit complete;
**registered development retention gate failed. This version stops here.**

The native panel completed all 50,048 advances. Independent replay passed for
all 17 logical cells, 18 segments and 154 published states. Faithful inheritance
passed all four world/pattern comparisons. Neither original SourceKernel nor
the new GrammarSourceKernel passed any complete retention comparison. The
registered grammar gate therefore fails, and held-out transfer and discovery
remain unlaunched. Audit passage establishes evidence integrity, not biological
acceptance.

## Fixed comparison

The [contract](../experiments/evolving_minds/contracts/ta1_mutation_stress_v1.md)
was fixed before execution. Exposed seeds 17 and 23 each received Alternating
and Blocks4 informative environments, with a faithful Delayed ancestor and three
Immediate useful arms: Faithful, original SourceKernel and GrammarSourceKernel.
The 64-tick horizon, B24 paid offspring endowment, prices, native developmental
rules and initial homes were matched. A separate grammar recovery control stopped
at committed tick 24 and resumed in a fresh process.

Both mutation kernels retained a 3/4 mutation gate. The new kernel uniformly
samples changed single-source edits passing D0 syntax, without filtering material
reachability, behavior or fitness. This family's neighborhood contains 14 legal
alternatives: identity probability 1/4, each alternative 3/56. Host syntax checking
is declared scaffolding. It is not an evolved or natively paid mutation policy.

Every useful arm required at least one additional active completed offspring,
at least eight additional units of net resource gain, and a path of three paid
functional parent-child transmissions in **each** founder family. Each path member
had to complete development, make at least four paid attempts and succeed on at
least 3/4 of its attempts. The supplied founder is generation 0. Genotype changes
are permitted along qualifying paths. Grammar had to pass all four comparisons;
SourceKernel was a fixed comparator, not a selectable fallback.

Faithful copying sustained five functional paid transmissions in both families
in every cell. Lifecycle and funding can therefore support the required depth
when this supplied useful program is copied without variation.

Grammar eliminated syntax rejection but retained materially unreachable edits.
In seed 17, each family's longest qualifying path included one changed-genotype
edge and reached two paid transmissions. This demonstrates short functional
retention across genotype change, while missing the fixed three-transmission
criterion. Both seed-17 grammar cells passed the offspring and resource margins
but failed both persistence predicates. In seed 23, all four paid grammar births
were unreachable and failed development, producing no active offspring. Both
seed-23 grammar cells failed offspring and persistence criteria; Blocks4 also
failed the resource margin. Every failed and censored outcome remains included.

SourceKernel also failed the complete screen: one seed-17 family reached depth
three while the other reached zero; seed-23 depths were zero and one. Removing
syntax rejection alone was insufficient in this fixed comparison. Rejected
Source proposals and accepted but unreachable Grammar proposals have different
native payment/refund consequences, which the net resource endpoints retain.
The panel does not establish that one kernel is generally superior.

## Decision and limits

TA-1 v1 stops before held-out evaluation, discovery, candidate extraction or
cumulative reuse. The reserved eight evaluation blocks / 96 cells remain
unopened by this work. All scientific acceptance, transfer, discovery and
cumulative-reuse flags remain false. The earlier temporal development result
remains valid, but does not bypass this failed gate.

The supported conclusion is narrow: **faithful useful inheritance works here;
the tested 75% source-mutation regimes did not sustain the required useful
lineages, even with syntax filtering.** Material constructibility remains a
distinct constraint from grammar validity. The result does not test every
mutation rate, representation, repair mechanism or environment, and cannot
establish a universal hypergraph/Hypersim ceiling. It does not demonstrate
autonomous discovery or evolution of mutation policy.

Any future change to rate, representation, construction rules or proposal
filtering requires a separately versioned prospective question and contract.
This result is not a reason to lower the present threshold, add favorable seeds,
protect the known useful edit or continue the existing lineage until it passes.
