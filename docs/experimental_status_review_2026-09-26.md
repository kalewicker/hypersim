# Experimental status and continuation review — 2026-09-26

The highest-value continuation is a coordinated programme of **damage-tolerant
rule maintenance, viable variation, and useful adaptation**. The recent ERE
work establishes several missing mechanisms. The preserved LK workspace adds
later self-modification and cognition results that materially change the older
root overview. Keeping each historical experiment active as its own campaign
would repeat questions already answered and spread effort across overlapping
objectives.

This is a review and recommendation, not a new preregistration or experiment
launch. Recommendations below do not change old gates, admit training, or imply
that the two Cargo workspaces should be merged.

## Evidence and scope

Reviewed the root architecture/status document, current ERE contracts, findings
and saved status/audit records, and the later findings preserved under
`experiments/lk/docs`. The latter is a separate implementation snapshot, not
another replicate of the root experiments. Nine relevant LK finding documents

The label-separated auditor was rerun through its read-only library API:
48 receipts, 3,072 steps, 8,640 world rewrites, 48 paid repairs, zero aliased
bindings and zero valid changed-body steps passed, along with 16 tamper controls.
No old receipts or checksum files were rewritten.

The checkpoint assay completed during this review. Its saved summary reports
13 baselines, 135 forks and two fresh-process resumes, totalling 6,428 advancing
steps, with training still unadmitted. The continuation result has a deliberately
limited same-build execution-core scope; it is not general runner restart,
optimizer-state persistence, or a new-profile semantic-gate pass.
Read-only comparisons independently matched every saved fork and child suffix
to its baseline, including terminal windows and guard ledgers, checked the
declared counts, and confirmed RNG consumption in the random control and
distinct parent/child process IDs. This checks the recorded comparisons, not
the full binary checkpoint decoder or a new execution of the assay.

The original inventory in `project_architecture_and_experiment_status.md` is

## 1. ERE and self-modification should share a research roadmap

### What has genuinely changed

The E2 pooled classification pass at the explicit 400k/50k envelope resolved
previously unmeasured classifications. Its downstream gate association remains
specific to that profile and its narrow 2/45 library envelope. It cannot admit
the later witness/completeness profiles automatically.

The grammar and maintenance pilots remained negative on unprotected whole-family
maintenance. The joint score improved measurement, but 10/16 original A3
families became invalid with no recovery. Increasing optimizer budget would
not directly address that missing mechanism.

Subsequent experiments supplied concrete capabilities:

- One-use activation survives compilation and changes actual world rewrites
  under the protected control.
- Typed witness repair restores damaged families in the bounded exposed fixture.
- The complete-witness codec rejects the composite erasure that the older reader
  could accept as a changed program. The old failed result remains failed.
- Repeated damage establishes finite, paid exact recovery. Label-separated
  motifs establish distinct-node topology changes and class pause/resume around
  quarantine and repaired compilation.
- The newly completed checkpoint assay records continuation equivalence across
  damaged, repaired, exhausted and ownership-loss boundaries, plus an RNG/window
  control and two fresh-process resumes.

The latest topology trial is four constructed two-state oscillators. Four seeds
produce identical outcomes without engine RNG consumption. They demonstrate
deterministic mechanism reproducibility, not four independent ecological samples
or learned adaptation. Fuel exhaustion stops recovery; ownership loss remains
unrepairable even with fuel left.

### The overlap with the later LK selfmod experiments

Selfmod v10 documents 7/8 clonal survivors at 20,000 steps under keyed-random
scheduling with zero match pricing, while all tested nonzero mutation rates
collapse. Novel rule classes appear before collapse. This supports a
variation-quality/capital-loss diagnosis under that instrument; it does not
establish that every possible mutation operator is nonviable.

The common question is now clear: **can a rule population remain operational
while admitting useful change?** Repair handles corruption and ERE's coherent
representation; the selfmod spectrum assay can identify viable intentional
changes. Exact restoration alone cannot supply evolutionary improvement.

Keep the implementations isolated. Share experimental definitions, candidate
designs and comparison tables first. Any future port must preserve each
lineage's matching, scheduling, economics and semantic-profile identity.

### Recommended sequence

1. Finish recording/verifying the completed continuation result and run the
   complete-witness profile's own full semantic gates. Keep optimizer readiness
   and checkpointing distinct from the execution-core continuation result.
2. Run one bounded mutation-spectrum assay in the corrected selfmod instrument:
   operator by parent, single edit, decode/validity, actual firing, productive
   rate and capital outcome. Include parent and unchanged-clone controls. Make
   this diagnostic before introducing probation or another economy change.
3. Design a small sustained-maintenance fixture. Compare protection, exposed
   no-repair, exposed finite repair, and an explicitly new mechanism through
   which world work earns maintenance resources. Log every debit/credit and
   representation cost. A supply driven only by a clock tests externally
   subsidized repair; it does not establish dependence on useful world work.
4. Add one viable intentional editor and one changing task only after that
   maintenance fixture works. Under the complete codec, legitimate edits need
   explicit native operations that preserve/update ownership declarations;
   the current repair catalogue does not provide arbitrary structural editing.
   Measure adaptation by held-out world performance against a frozen programme.

The older pilot required every family valid on every step and no quarantine.
The new repair trajectory necessarily includes temporary invalidity: for
example, the exposed fuel8/no-ownership topology trial is exact-valid on 60/64
steps. It does **not** pass the old gate. A new damage-tolerance contract should
predeclare recovery latency, productive availability, terminal loss and energy
criteria, while preserving the old pilot's failure. Historical score products
also need component-wise reporting; a positive product can hide lost families.

Pause broader training if the new mechanism only preserves the seeded oscillator,
resource supply is independent of world work, ownership loss dominates, or no
productive variants are available. Those findings still answer useful questions.

## 2. Cognition is worth continuing with a better task

The root's StaticBias/F2 negative is not the latest cognition result. The LK
D/E phases document trained MlpScorer champions above all 30 sampled untrained
directions on four substrates, by 4.9%–10.0% over the best sampled direction.
That is meaningful, modest learning within the tested objective/class.

The E4 details narrow the closeout headline: `relay_tri` exceeds untrained at
the stated bar, but its champion-minus-budget-matched-random result is only
1.84 sigma. It should not be presented as a decisive random-baseline win on
every substrate. The later absolute graph, EI and lattice measurements also
show that much of the F5 advantage comes from smaller, more repetitive worlds.
Reuse success does not establish increasingly rich cognition or organization.

Use this policy machinery as a tool for the shared adaptive-world programme:
define useful work and a held-out environment change, measure raw performance
and required structure, then compare champion, untrained, budget-matched random,
and a simple hand heuristic. First wire the already-built multiple-draw
prechecks into the campaign runner. Predeclare headroom and baseline checks
before spending another full campaign budget.

Retire the old Stage 0b separation table until a real controlled perturbation
exists; adjacent PRNG seeds are independent draws. Keep the proposed F6
emergence-correction campaign closed: its motivating causal chain was falsified.
Do not use CE or constructor concentration alone as a richer-behavior target.

## 3. Locality deserves a bounded closure/generality decision

The root overview's “need an age mechanism” boundary is superseded in the LK
lineage: passive birth-step age gating and EXP-L18 already demonstrate
age-controlled coexistence. Reintroducing a countdown rule would repeat the
observer back-action problem. The corrected balance description depends on
realized production rate and residual contention, not lifetime alone.

The unresolved closure question is narrower. The long runs contain a fill
ramp, a plateau and a budget-driven quench. Updating the population census did
not rescue stationarity. The latest preregistered rate band should be applied
without selecting a pleasing segment after seeing the data. Effective sample
size and stationarity must pass together before any CD estimate is claimed.
LK seeds vary sampled instruments, not the deterministic dynamics.

The snapshot contains an influx implementation, but no completed sustained-
influx scientific result was found in the reviewed findings. First repair the
inherited positional CSV test readers and exact-float audit assertion in a
separate, documented follow-up. Then a prospectively defined replenished-energy
experiment could test whether the finite-budget quench is the blocker. Such
influx is an explicit change of physics and requires an extended ledger.

If locality is the near-term publication target, prioritize its one-axis
generality tests and close the present paper at the attractor/coexistence and
back-action claims. Treat the metronome/contention identity as a bounded
mechanistic question. Avoid commissioning another long CD run until a short
preregistered feasibility study shows that its sample and stationarity floors
can both be met. A trajectory-level CD, if obtained, is not a family-level
initial-condition ensemble result.

## 4. Keep settled tracks as controls and tools

**ALife 5–5e:** stop trying further cost or mortality knobs against a threshold
that no longer affects behavior. Continue only with a genuinely new trait-task
coupling or resource trade-off. Reuse replication, turnover and lineage
accounting as benchmark infrastructure for adaptive rule ecology.

**Alphabet A–E:** stop extending the same fixed-rule sweeps. Their saturation
results form the baseline for endogenous rule change. Report recurring,
substrate-visible activity and its time/event denominators; serial relabels,
meta-band material and raw class accumulation are insufficient evidence of
functional novelty. A finite richer vocabulary is a feasibility step, not a
claim of open-endedness.

**GE fees and ERE F5-prime:** these are different outer loops despite the R09
name collision. Fee containment is a useful mechanism. Its later C2 paired
grid completed three seeds, lost one cell to a compute kill and left another
seed unrun; conditional results must remain conditional. The F5-prime learning
loop did not beat initialization at its tested small budget and exposes
single-class concentration as a scoring failure mode. Keep it as a diagnostic,
not the default next optimization target. Future explosive-loop work should
log explicit compute/memory ceilings and censored observations.

**CE/V-track:** retain the completed instrument validations and scoped negative
results. Reopen CE only for a new biological/dynamical question with a fixed
observable and valid estimation window. LK ERE R6's frequent rule changes
prevented its finite-cadence stationarity intersection; later adaptation does
not automatically make EI/CE estimable. Always report absolute micro/macro EI,
state counts, scope filters and denominators alongside ratios.

## Recommended allocation

Concentrate new experimental work on ERE maintenance and the selfmod mutation
spectrum, with cognition supplying a later task-directed optimizer. Keep one
bounded locality closeout/generality task active if that claim is a current
priority. Treat ALife, alphabet and causal instruments as reusable controls
until the new world supplies a question they can actually discriminate.

The next substantial scientific milestone is **productive persistence under
exposure with useful, affordable change**. It is stricter than accumulating
classes or keeping an oscillator alive, and it brings the overlapping
experiments onto a common, testable trajectory.
