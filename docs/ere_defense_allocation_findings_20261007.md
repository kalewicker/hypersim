## Outcome and scope

The native fixture completes all16 cells ×32 =512 advances plus16 paid
non-advancing Seal initializations. Corrected frozen historical accounting
passes all seven diagnostic criteria and six allocation test groups; the final
completion archive also passes six unchanged survival groups and feature-off
checking. No simulation retry, optimizer update or criterion change occurs.

This task has an actual state-dependent opportunity cost: defend is uniquely
better under a threat with a low offered bounty; harvest is uniquely better
under a high bounty or no threat. The fixed-table adaptive comparator averages
12.5 utility versus11 for always harvesting and8 for always defending. This is
task headroom, not a trained policy or a scientific superiority estimate.

The existing encoder cannot yet use this task. Its ordinary-node projection
aliases all eight wallet/forecast/bounty states, and its public observation
extractor only admits initialization snapshots, not the step13 decision graph.
The next concrete extension is a versioned resource-token observation and
binary-action adapter, not another fit of the unchanged encoder.

Original postflight accounting fails and remains failed. Two intermediate
accounting/completion attempts also fail on historical test packaging and
remain intact; the final separately sealed completion verifies the corrected
accounting without new native work. These distinctions are part of the result,
not erased by the final positive verification.

All `scientific_acceptance`, `sustained_claim`, `training_admitted` and
`independent_physics_replay` flags remainfalse. Birth is explicitly authored
correct actions in one familiar world; reliable forecast, voucher utility,
allocation rules and threat schedule are engineered. No learned choice,
endogenous defense, new ecological resource source, unseen-state transfer,

## Native mechanism and conserved unit ledger

Two paid birth edits at1/5 create the specified useful rule, installed at6.
At13 the native graph observation is saved before allocation, with its full
inventory and wallet/threat/payoff token identities. It exposes wallet units,
forecast bit and offered bounty, not the chosen action or optimum. Paired
defend/harvest observation inventories are byte-value equal within each state.

At14, a native allocation rewrite either transfers one wallet unit to defense
escrow or consumes it for the offered2/12 graph-resident voucher credits.
Native harvest catalogues match the bounty property; no host callback creates
credits. Wallet0 admits neither allocation. At15 a native forecast-guarded
issuer creates a pending threat; at16 damage and escrow-funded absorption
compete for the same designated sealed payload. The losing damage binding is
admitted and independently checked even when absorption wins. No blanket META
protection, free repair, original-program restore cache or replenishment.

Funded defense under a threat burns escrow at16 and preserves the class.
Funded safe defense keeps one unused escrow unit and forgoes the bounty;
there is no late salvage. Harvest has no defense unit and loses the class
under a threat, but preserves it in safe states. Unpaid controls spend no
wallet, earn no voucher credits and absorb no threat.

At every step, wallet + escrow + absorbed/burned units + harvest-spent units
equals the initial wallet. Across all16 cells, initial wallet units total8:
two burned defense units, two unspent safe escrow units, four harvest-spent
units, zero final wallet units. Voucher credits are authored utility, not
fungible fuel or conserved physical energy.

Aggregate native ledger:32 birth payments,8 allocation payments,8 issued
threats,2 absorptions,6 first damaging lesions, zero repeated erases or repair,
28 harvested credits and16 Seal payments. Class identity, initial-class
exclusion, distinct ordinary bindings and actual pre-step tables are audited.
