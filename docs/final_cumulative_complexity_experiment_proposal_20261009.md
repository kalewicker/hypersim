# A final bounded test of cumulative evolution in a self-modifying hypergraph

Date: 2026-10-09. Status: accepted design direction; implementation started at
the bounded readiness phase. This proposal is not the scientific preregistration
or campaign launch contract. No existing scientific verdict changes.

The first integrated capacity fixture is implemented in `experiments/cumulative`.
See `docs/cumulative_readiness_findings_20261009.md` for its frozen inputs,
capacity results, independent verification, and the remaining readiness work.
The shared-resource population continuation also passes its bounded engineering
fixture; see `docs/cumulative_shared_population_readiness_findings_20261009.md`.
Births now transfer existing capital and grant zero environmental resources,
with retained death/site/funding failures and independently audited scheduling.
The complete readiness gate and scientific campaign remain unadmitted.
The lineage/intervention measurement continuation is implemented and independently
verified in `docs/cumulative_lineage_intervention_readiness_findings_20261009.md`.
It recovers authored causal controls while keeping founder material, external
edits, dead samples and failed development evidence distinct from discovery.

## Decision this experiment should support

Can a reproducing hypergraph population autonomously acquire, transmit, and
build upon useful changes to its own executable rules, under finite resources?

A positive result would demonstrate bounded cumulative functional evolution in
this designed world. A negative result would justify retiring this configuration
and ending this experimental programme at the agreed budget. Neither outcome
would settle whether arbitrary hypergraph systems can support open-ended evolution.

The proposal replaces parallel capability-building tracks with one integrated
campaign and its controls. It does not merge their historical evidence or import
their positive claims as acceptance evidence for the new experiment.

## Proposed world

Use one isolated experimental workspace and one explicit physical contract.
Reuse compatible implementation components, not assumptions that the LK and
root ERE runtimes are interchangeable. Resolve that integration choice before
writing the executable campaign contract; a repository-wide rewrite is unnecessary.

Start with a small viable reproducing founder population. Its executable rules
and heritable editor machinery are represented in the graph. The initial organism
has a minimal resource pathway and generic editing operations, not a catalogue
of solution programs. Engine laws, accounting, ownership boundaries and the
instruction vocabulary remain fixed; organisms can change their own programs
within that vocabulary. This is designed reproduction, not a test of life's origin.

Provide a finite externally driven resource supply with local depletion,
competition, maintenance costs and paid reproduction. Primitive local rewrite
reactions should admit multiple resource-processing routes and intermediate
products. Opportunities are present from the beginning; no experimenter unlocks
a new task when a lineage improves. Resource inflow and any variation are fixed
independently of performance. A stable simple niche must remain possible, so
failure to increase complexity is a legitimate outcome rather than automatic death.

Pay for resource extraction, execution, storage, copying and edits using the
declared economy. Population change follows those native outcomes; there is no
external score rewarding node count, novelty, pathway depth or complexity.
Measurements never enter organism decisions or reproductive selection.

Edits in the main arm must be actual local operations generated by graph-resident
machinery, with parent-child transmission of the resulting program. Randomness
may supply blind variation, but an external script must not choose beneficial
targets, insert the next innovation, optimize controllers or rescue a lineage.
All arms receive the same independently specified environment law; realized
resources may diverge because organisms alter their environment.

This world is proposed work. Existing fixtures do not establish that its economy,
editor semantics, alternate routes or evolutionary accessibility already exist.

## Primary meaning of complexity

Use **cumulative, heritable, causally useful program organization**. A qualifying
lineage must acquire at least two successive functional innovations after the
founder; the second must build on retained machinery from the first. One lucky
mutation, a return to the founder, a neutral appendage or two unrelated switches
does not satisfy this definition.

For every prospectively selected candidate lineage, require:

1. Authenticated endogenous edit history and actual paid descent from its founder.
2. New executable organization used in ordinary resource acquisition or maintenance.
3. Transmission and useful activity across a frozen minimum number of generations.
4. A later innovation that depends on an earlier acquired mechanism, demonstrated
   by prospective endpoint intervention rules, not inferred from graph size.
5. A net native reproductive/resource benefit in standardized comparison assays,
   with storage, edit and execution costs included.

Measure total graph size, executable program size, active rule dependencies and
resource-processing depth separately as secondary descriptors. None alone is
the primary criterion. Causal ablation supplies a tested lower bound on useful
organization; it does not prove globally minimal program size or irreducibility.

Freeze candidate selection before evolution (for example a deterministic sample
of extant lineages at fixed census times). Avoid choosing only the best surviving
endpoint. Include extinct, noninnovating and capped worlds in the world denominator.
Multiple lineages or observation windows within a world are not independent worlds.

## Comparison arms

| Arm | What it tests |
|---|---|
| Heritable endogenous editing | Full proposed mechanism |
| External blind mutation at reproduction, no endogenous editing | Whether ordinary evolution explains any gain without self-editing; freeze a resource/opportunity budget prospectively, not a success-matched number of edits |
| Endogenous edits that are not transmitted | Whether within-lifetime changes suffice; implement explicit reproduction semantics and matched copying costs |
| Frozen heritable founder program | Ecological growth, sorting and fixed-program adaptation without new inherited programs |

Run arms in separate worlds initialized under the same registered world-seed
design. Use separate reproducible environment, edit and scheduling random streams
where applicable; never force event-by-event parity after populations diverge.
Document unavoidable intervention cost differences rather than calling the arms
perfectly matched. Successful repair or inheritance controls alone are not success.

At fixed endpoints, evaluate sampled programs and registered ancestral versions
in shared standardized environments, with mutation disabled for measurement.
Include ablation, sham ablation and restoration comparisons. These diagnostic
branches never feed back into the evolving population. Reserve new environmental
draws under the same laws for confirmation; withhold outcomes from all development.
Ablation protocols must control for generic malformed-program damage and allow
for redundant mechanisms rather than requiring every single rule to be essential.

## One readiness gate, one scientific campaign

Before freezing the campaign, finish one bounded integrated readiness gate on
development worlds only. It must establish:

- A hand-authored multistage capacity witness earns a native net benefit over the
  founder, survives its costs and can transmit its program. It is never seeded
  into scientific populations or used as a training target.
- Legal paid local edits can reach that witness from the founder through viable
  intermediates; this rules out an obvious disconnected search space, not an
  evolutionary waiting-time problem.
- Founder viability, all arm semantics, resource accounting, edit provenance,
  scheduler opportunity accounting and checkpoint continuation are testable.
- Identity relabeling and candidate-order probes do not silently create the
  purported advantage. Binding enumeration caps and their consequences are explicit.
- The entire planned campaign and endpoint interventions fit a measured resource
  budget, including storage and independent verification.

Set a time/effort cap on this gate before implementation. If it cannot pass
within that cap, report that this final design was not made testable and close
it; do not turn the gate into another unbounded sequence of experimental tracks.

After readiness, freeze the world law, founder, operators, costs, arms, independent
world count, seeds, horizons, candidate selection, intervention suite, acceptance
thresholds and compute/storage limits together. Choose the replicate count and
minimum accepted success rate through an explicit sensitivity/power calculation,
not a convenient seed count. No scientific launch is specified by this draft.

Run one campaign. Keep all worlds, failed routes, cap hits and original outcomes.
No seed replacement, outcome-dependent extensions, warm starts from successful
lineages, threshold relaxation or new tasks after inspecting the results.

## Decision and closure

| Outcome | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Cumulative criterion passes across the required independent worlds and endogenous editing improves on the registered comparison | Evidence for cumulative functional evolution with a causal contribution from self-editing in this world |
| Cumulative criterion passes, but external blind mutation performs similarly | Evolutionary complexity is demonstrated within the design; an additional advantage from self-editing is not established |
| Survival, growth, novelty or one useful innovation without cumulative passage | Partial capability; the primary question fails |
| Valid campaign with no cumulative passage | Negative result within the frozen world, mechanism and budget; retire this configuration |
| Invalid accounting, unmeasured work or binding operational failure | Inconclusive scientific result; still close the funded attempt rather than silently retrying |

Passing means a finite instance of cumulative evolution, never proof of
open-endedness. Failure supports a programme-level decision to stop investing
in this configuration, never a universal impossibility claim about hypergraphs.
