## Candidate question and required engineering changes

Use five-instruction founders and truthful immediate cues. The delayed program provides an explicit, weaker authored starting policy. The same-length immediate and coin policies provide headroom/chance controls. The result would concern inherited resource-choice adaptation in this fixed language and ecology. It would not establish learning, memory, general cognition or superiority over direct encoding.

Check that calculation and the funded control under a separate native contract. The founder-once exclusion used in the diagnostic must be absent. Calibrate disjoint/competing admissions and opportunity exposure under the revised budget. ## Candidate fixed campaign

Five arms per world:

1. **Inherited variation:** delayed-cue founders; SourceKernel on every admitted request.
2. **Frozen inheritance:** identical delayed-cue founders; faithful copying on every request.
3. **Unevolved variant:** each founder samples one SourceKernel proposal at its first admitted request; retain that proposal for its later founder requests, with faithful copying by descendants. Invalid proposals remain invalid and charged; no substitute genotype or repeated search. Record the retained founder proposal and all applications. This is a separate explicit comparator mechanism requiring native/audit implementation.
4. **Matched immediate-cue positive control:** the five-instruction program with instruction 103 source changed from state slot 2 to sensor slot 0; faithful copying. Keep the extra state and wire and their costs.
5. **Matched coin control:** the five-instruction program with instruction 103 source changed to sensor slot 1; faithful copying. Keep the same material envelope and prices.

All founders receive the same initial reserve and site budgets, perform paid development, and reproduce under the same physical eligibility and supplied scheduler. Comparator policies are authored controls. They are not evolutionary results. Environmental cue/resource/site coin schedules are shared per world; mutation/operator streams are separately documented semantic streams. Shared world seed does not force divergent endogenous events to remain paired.

The proposal comprises 20 development runs and 40 held-out runs, 60 registered runs total. Development results can support an openly declared design revision; they cannot count as held-out passage. Unexpected engineering failures or resource censoring stop interpretation and remain in the denominator. They cannot be replaced by successful worlds.

## Candidate primary endpoint and effect criteria

Primary endpoint per arm/world: cumulative harvested energy from organisms born after the initial founders, over every one of the 64 ticks. Exclude founder harvest in every arm; include all descendant lineages and retain zero yield from rejected/extinct variants. Charge and independently reconstruct every development, attempt, maintenance, request, copy and child endowment. Report births, deaths, lineage persistence, genotype/phenotype changes, paid construction, invalidity, opportunity exposure and total budgets as secondary diagnostics.

Two primary paired comparisons: inherited variation minus frozen inheritance, and inherited variation minus unevolved variant, over all eight held-out worlds. Candidate passage requires, for **both** comparisons:

- Mean paired yield improvement at least 64 energy units per world.
- Positive paired difference in all eight registered worlds; ties and nonpositive differences do not count as positive.
- Exact one-sided sign-tail probability at most 0.025 for each comparison, giving a conservative two-comparison family threshold. With eight worlds, eight positive differences give `1/256=0.00390625`; seven give `9/256=0.03515625` and fail.
- Complete uncensored execution, independent reconstruction and the preregistered paid-construction/opportunity/control checks.

This stringent eight-world design has limited power. The 64-unit effect floor and seed count are proposal choices, not thresholds established by the current one-world calibration. Review feasibility and analysis on the fixed development worlds before freezing a final preregistration; any changed design must have a new version and fresh, prospectively fixed held-out worlds if the old ones have been exposed. A positive point estimate alone is not passage. A successful test would still be scoped to these fixed worlds and the declared generation process.

The matched immediate and coin controls test task headroom and chance behavior. Define their required feasibility/opportunity checks numerically in the final calibration contract before observing held-out results. They cannot become alternate primary comparators after a failed primary analysis.
