## What the calibration actually ran

The [prospective contract](../experiments/evolving_minds/contracts/d0_neighborhood_v1.md) fixed 58 cells: all 21 single-source edits of `cue`, all 35 of `delayed_cue`, and one unchanged control for each. Every cell completed 64 ticks (167040 native advances total), including extinct tails. World seed 17 and the existing mutation-v1 prices were unchanged.

At the first lawful native birth request, the assay installed the enumerated source edit once. Syntax rejection paid charge 1 and refunded the unused escrow. Valid syntax copied a seed child with reserve 12 and charged the usual copying fee. There was no redraw, repair or substitute child. Thereafter a declared native birth-ID >=2 guard excluded the original founder from reproduction, while every descendant copied faithfully. The founder continued ordinary behavior/upkeep until death. This prevents repeated founder sibling seeding in this diagnostic; it differs from admission-v1's supplied parallel scheduler and does not establish reproductive fairness.

The assay uses bounded full traces, appropriate for a small fixture. Its special diagnostic state is explicitly unsupported by old checkpoint formats, and checkpoint export rejects it. Scientific evolution on a future funded profile will require that profile's own live-checkpoint integration and resource pilot.

The unchanged cue control's first child was born at tick 3, completed three paid instructions, committed 14 attempts and succeeded on all 14. It harvested 112 units, paid 45.75 maintenance, reproduced and died by age at tick 19. Its lineage produced 25 further descendants, retained eight live inherited organisms at tick 64 and harvested 2392 units. The sole grammar-reachable edited cue program changed the Wire source from cue to coin; it completed construction, earned no reward on its first paid attempt in this exposed world, and died empty without reproducing. This is not evidence that coin organisms cannot survive on other worlds.

The unchanged delayed-cue child was born at tick 5, paid three development instructions and nine maintenance units, then died empty at tick 8, age three. It never halted, attempted an action, harvested energy or reproduced. All four grammar-reachable edits also failed to complete paid construction. Source-only edits preserve instruction count and opcodes, so they cannot shorten this development pipeline. Some unreachable edits are censored by energy exhaustion before the invalid instruction executes; grammar unreachability and observed paid developmental failure are distinct fields.

The failure is a price/endowment mismatch, not missing accounting. A five-instruction seed starts with upkeep 2.625. The normal first three construction steps add state, actuator and wire material. Full payment would require `(1+2.875)+(1+3.125)+(1+3.375)=12.375` units before any halted-program action is possible. The child receives only 12. Maintenance exhausts the remaining reserve and native death settles the balance. This explains why even the unchanged, grammar-reachable genotype cannot establish inherited descent. Every native accounting row has zero residual.

## Task headroom and terminology correction

The resource Boolean specifies which of two actions is rewarded. It does not specify whether a reward exists. An attempted choice equal to the signal earns 8 units for either Boolean value, provided ordinary funding/site guards permit settlement; every attempt costs 1. The independent interpreter and native rules both enforce this equality. A truthful immediate cue therefore lets an already developed cue-following program make the correct choice at every paid opportunity. Selection from that authored policy cannot demonstrate improved use of information in this task.

Delayed cue has a longer response path, and a source edit can route the actuator directly to cue. That is potential headroom within the language, but the current child reserve prevents the five-instruction offspring from reaching the comparison. Increasing funded construction in a separately declared profile is the next feasibility test. It must preserve parent payment and fixed accounting; this review does not change the old reserve, copying prices, task or archived criteria.
